Questions

Asked every day.

Is the price really one-time?+
Yes. $800 for one person or $1,400 for a couple covers the entire program — there's no monthly fee to me. The only recurring cost is your Smart Credit monitoring subscription, which must stay paid and active while I'm working your file. It's billed separately by SmartCredit.com and it's how I pull all three of your reports every month.
What counts as a couple?+
Any two people who want their files worked together — spouses, partners, a parent and adult child, two business partners. Each person needs their own Smart Credit account so I can see all six reports.
How long until my score moves?+
The bureaus have 30 days to respond to each dispute round, so most clients see their first deletions and score movement within 30–45 days. Full programs typically run 4–6 months depending on the file. Every file is different — results vary.
Is this legal?+
Completely. The Fair Credit Reporting Act gives you the right to dispute anything inaccurate, unverifiable or untimely on your reports. I do it professionally and by the book through ImproveU LLC, a registered credit services organization in Fresno, CA.
