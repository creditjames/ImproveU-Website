After Enrollment

What happens after you enroll

  1. 01

    Select Your Plan

    Choose the tier that fits your credit goals and budget. Every plan is a one-time payment with no hidden fees or contracts.

  2. 02

    Activate Credit Monitoring

    Sign up for SmartCredit ($1 to start) so we can pull your full 3-bureau report and identify every negative item on your file.

  3. 03

    Complete Onboarding

    Submit your government-issued ID, Social Security number, and proof of address so we can legally act on your behalf.

  4. 04

    We Start Removing

    Our team begins filing disputes, escalations, and legal challenges to the bureaus and creditors immediately.

Your credit repair process cannot begin until your credit monitoring is activated and your onboarding documents are submitted. Incomplete setups will delay your file. Please complete both steps as soon as possible after enrollment to avoid any holdups.

CROA Disclosure

Under the Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA), you have the right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report by contacting the credit bureau directly. No one can legally remove accurate, timely, and verifiable information from your credit report. You have the right to obtain a copy of your credit report from a credit bureau. You may cancel your contract with ImproveU LLC, without penalty or obligation, within 5 business days from the date you signed it.
