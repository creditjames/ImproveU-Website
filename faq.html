The process

Every file is different. Some clients see results in 30–45 days, others take 3–6 months. It depends on how many items need to be disputed, how the bureaus and creditors respond, and the complexity of your file. We work aggressively from day one.

We challenge inaccurate, unverifiable, and non-compliant items including late payments, collections, charge-offs, repossessions, foreclosures, bankruptcies, judgments, medical bills, student loans, public records, hard inquiries, and identity errors. All items are challenged based on accuracy, compliance, and applicable consumer protection laws.

Absolutely. Credit repair is 100% legal and protected under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), and the Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA). You have the legal right to dispute any inaccurate information on your credit report.

We handle everything. You don’t write letters, call bureaus, or track responses. Our team manages your entire file — disputes, escalations, follow-ups, and strategy adjustments. You just monitor your progress through our app.

05 / 06

Sending disputes

Depending on your plan, disputes are sent via standard mail (Tier 1) or certified mail with tracking (Tier 2 & 3). Certified mail creates a legal paper trail that adds pressure to bureaus and creditors to respond properly.

Under the FCRA, bureaus have 30 days to investigate a dispute. If they don’t respond within that window, the item must legally be removed from your report. We track every deadline and escalate when bureaus fail to comply.
