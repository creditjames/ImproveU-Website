The Process

Five phases. One outcome.

Each one builds on the last. This is how we dismantle inaccurate reporting and rebuild your credit profile from the ground up.

  1. 01

    Analyze

    We pull your full credit file across all 3 bureaus and review every account, balance, date, and status line by line. No automation — just trained eyes finding every error and vulnerability.

    All 3 bureaus · line by line
  2. 02

    Strategize

    Your file gets a custom dispute strategy based on what we find. We identify which items to challenge first, which laws apply, and map the fastest path to measurable improvement.

    Custom dispute strategy
  3. 03

    Dispute

    We draft targeted, law-backed dispute letters and send them via certified mail with return receipt. Every letter references specific FCRA provisions and demands verified documentation.

    Certified mail · return receipt
  4. 04

    Escalate

    If bureaus or creditors stall, we escalate. CFPB complaints, attorney general filings, and secondary disputes citing FDCPA and CROA violations — real legal pressure, not empty threats.

    CFPB · Attorney General filings
  5. 05

    Rebuild

    As negatives fall off, we help you add positive tradelines, optimize utilization, and build a credit profile designed for approvals — mortgages, auto loans, business funding, and more.
